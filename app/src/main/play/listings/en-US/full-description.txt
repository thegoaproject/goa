GOA plays classic MS-DOS games on Android. It sets each game up for you: controls, sound card, speed and the game's own settings, so you can just press Play.

SET UP FOR YOU
• Controller layouts, sound cards and game settings are tuned per game, and we keep refining them with feedback from players.
• Touch controls named after the game's own actions, or use a gamepad with buttons you can remap.
• Video filters from clean and modern to a classic CRT look.

YOUR GAMES
• Free classics included: freeware favourites, released by their makers for anyone to play. One tap to install.
• Your GOG.com library: sign in with GOG to install the DOS games you own, straight onto your phone.
• Your own files: add a folder and GOA finds the DOS games in it, including every folder inside it, or import a game archive.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Save Slot and Load Slot save your exact place in any game, on top of the game's own saves.
• Your library is grouped by where each game came from, with search, sorting and filters.
• Works on phones, tablets and foldables, in light or dark theme.

FREE TO TRY
The free version includes every feature and every free classic, plus 3 of your own games (from GOG or your own files) installed at a time. GOA Pro is a one-time purchase that removes that limit and supports development. No subscription, no ads.

PRIVACY
Nothing is shared unless you say so. Your GOG sign-in stays on your phone.

GOA doesn't include commercial games: you play the ones you own. GOA is not affiliated with GOG.com. Questions or problems: support@goodoldandroid.app
