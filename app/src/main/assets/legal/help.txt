SUPPORT

Questions, problems or requests: email support@goodoldandroid.app. Include your phone model and the game's name if it's about a game.

A GAME DOESN'T WORK PROPERLY
Email us with the game's name and your phone model. If the game has a **SETUP** button, its own setup can change the sound card and other settings, but GOA has already set them up for you, so only try it if something isn't working.

GOA PRO AND PURCHASES
- Bought GOA Pro but it isn't unlocked? Go to **☰ → Account & Privacy → Restore purchase** while signed in to the same Google account.
- Refunds are handled by Google Play. See [Google Play's refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

DELETE YOUR DATA
- **Shared game health data:** in the app, go to **☰ → Account & Privacy → Delete my shared data**. This deletes everything shared under your current and previous player IDs. You can also email us your player ID (shown in ☰ → Account & Privacy) and we'll delete it.
- **Your GOG sign-in:** While you're signed in, tap **Sign Out** in the GOG account section at the top of **☰ → Account & Privacy** to remove it from your phone. GOA keeps no copy of it anywhere else. Your GOG account itself is managed by GOG.com.
- **Everything else:** games, saves and settings only exist on your phone. Uninstalling GOA removes them.

GOA has no accounts of its own, so there is no GOA account to delete. See the [Privacy Policy](https://goodoldandroid.app/privacy/) for details.
